Hop & Doodle Privacy Policy for iPhone and iPad

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Hop & Doodle is made by DoodleDev. It is a coloring and drawing app for children. It is designed to work entirely on your device and does not collect any information.

Information we collect

None. Hop & Doodle does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. The app does not connect to the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What stays on the device

Children's names, avatars, drawings, finished pictures and any photos added to the app are saved only in the app's private storage on the device. The app never uploads them. They are left out of the device's iCloud and computer backups unless a grown-up turns on Back up drawings in the grown-up settings. Then they are included in those backups, which stay under the device owner's account, and copies can remain in older backups after it is turned off. Uninstalling the app deletes them from the device.

Photos and camera

A photo can be added to turn into a coloring page. It is chosen with the system's own photo picker, which gives Hop & Doodle only that one photo, or taken with the device's camera, which the system asks a grown-up to allow first. It is kept on the device, and the app never sends it anywhere. A grown-up can delete all of a child's added pictures from that child's profile in the grown-up settings.

Saving pictures to the device

A grown-up can save a finished picture to the device's own photos, after the same grown-up check as the app's other settings. The system asks a grown-up to allow this the first time. The picture is then stored with the device's other photos and stays there even if the app is uninstalled. Hop & Doodle never sends it anywhere, but it is treated like any other photo: if the device syncs its photos, for example with iCloud Photos, the picture syncs too.

Ads, analytics and purchases

Hop & Doodle has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no third-party services, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Hop & Doodle is made for children. Because no information is collected, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page, and in the app under Privacy policy & licenses, with a new date.
