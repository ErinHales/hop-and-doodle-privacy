Hop & Doodle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Hop & Doodle is made by DoodleDev. It is a coloring and drawing app for children. It is designed to work entirely on your device and does not collect any information.

Information we collect

None. Hop & Doodle does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. The app has no internet access: it does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What stays on the device

Children's names, avatars, drawings, finished pictures and any photos added to the app are saved only in the app's private storage on the device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Photos and camera

A photo can be added to turn into a coloring page. It is chosen with Android's own photo picker or taken with the device's camera app, and only that one photo is shared with Hop & Doodle. It is kept on the device and never leaves it. A grown-up can delete all of a child's added pictures from that child's profile in the grown-up settings.

Saving pictures to the device

A grown-up can save a finished picture to the device's own photos, after the same grown-up check as the app's other settings. The picture is then stored with the device's other photos, in a Hop & Doodle album, and stays there even if the app is uninstalled. Hop & Doodle never sends it anywhere: the picture only goes where the grown-up takes it from the device's photos.

Sharing and rating the app

A grown-up can share a link to the app, with the app's own promo picture, or rate it, from the grown-up settings, or when the app asks for a rating after the grown-up check. These open the device's own share menu or the Google Play app, which then work on their own. Hop & Doodle itself still does not connect to the internet, and nothing about your child or their drawings is included.

Ads, analytics and purchases

Hop & Doodle has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no third-party services, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Hop & Doodle is made for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. Because no information is collected, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page, and in the app under Privacy policy & licenses, with a new date.
